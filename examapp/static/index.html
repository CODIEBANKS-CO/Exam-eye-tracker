Before you start

  • This exam needs your camera. If your computer or browser asks for permission to use the camera, choose Allow before you start. The exam cannot begin without it.
  • Sit in front of the camera with your face clearly visible and light in front of you.
  • Stay in full screen on this exam page. Switching to other windows is recorded.
  • One answer per question. You can go back and change answers until you submit or time runs out.
  • First the camera takes a few pictures of your face and keeps comparing you with them until the exam ends. Only you should be in view, and nobody else may take your place.
  • Then you look at a row of dots on the screen so the camera can learn where your eyes are. Keep your head still. The exam clock starts after that.
  • The timer is kept by the server and cannot be paused.
